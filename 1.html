What is a pun?


A pun is a joke. We switch a word with another word with the same sound but different meaning.



“Why was the math book sad?”
“Because it had too many problems.”


In this example, the word "problems" is the pun.
"Problem" can mean "a homework problem [in the book]" ([教科書にある]宿題の問題) or "a problem [in life]" ([人生の]問題).


Multiple meanings make it fun to play with language!
